How it works

Paste. Handle it. Post it.

Shotglass is a screenshot beautifier with real light. It turns a raw capture into an image ready for a launch thread, a changelog, a pull request, docs or a deck in about fifteen seconds, without leaving the browser tab you opened it in.

  1. Paste

    Press ⌘V or Ctrl V anywhere on the page, drop a file onto the stage, or tap to choose one. The shot lands on a backdrop cut from its own colors.

  2. Handle it

    Grab the shot and it tilts in real 3D, with weight and momentum until friction brings it to rest. Press anywhere on the backdrop and the light moves there; the shadow swings and a glare slides across the glass.

  3. Post it

    Copy the PNG to your clipboard, save it at up to 3×, share it from your phone, or drag the thumbnail into Slack, Figma or your desktop.

Every result travels

It leaves however you need it to.

  • The image

    A crisp PNG at 1×, 2× or 3×, straight to the clipboard or to a file. The clear backdrop keeps transparency.

  • The look link

    Your whole look rides in the URL. Send it to someone and their screenshot comes out styled the same way. Your image never goes in the link.

  • The CSS

    The same backdrop, tilt, corners and light-matched shadows as plain CSS, ready for a real <img> on your landing page.

  • The right size

    16:9 for X and YouTube, 1200 × 630 for link cards, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 stories, 4:3, or Auto to fit the shot exactly.

Private by construction

Your pixels never leave this tab.

  • No uploads

    Rendering runs on your own GPU through WebGL in a background thread. There is no server that could receive your image, so it never does.

  • Secrets erased, not blurred

    Erase replaces the pixels under an API key, an email or a name with what surrounds them. Blur and pixelation can sometimes be reversed; replaced pixels can’t.

  • No account, no tracking

    No sign-up, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party anything. The only thing Shotglass remembers is a look you star, kept in this browser until you forget it.

  • Works offline

    After the first visit the app caches itself. Lose your connection and it keeps working exactly the same.
